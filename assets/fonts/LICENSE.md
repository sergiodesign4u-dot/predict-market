# Fonts

The three families this product uses, served from this repo rather than from a
third-party CDN (see `components/fonts.css` for why).

| Family | Role | Designer | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Space Grotesk | display | Florian Karsten | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| DM Sans | body | Colophon Foundry, Jonny Pinhorn | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| IBM Plex Mono | numbers and mono | IBM, Bold Monday | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

The OFL permits bundling and serving the fonts with a work, including
commercially, provided the fonts are not sold on their own and any modified
version is renamed. These files are unmodified subsets (latin, latin-ext) as
produced by the Google Fonts API.
