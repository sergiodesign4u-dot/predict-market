3 Common patterns

PATTERN 01

Horizontal category nav + bottom tab bar

De-facto standard across HARD and SOFT groups. Polymarket (4 tabs), Kalshi (4 tabs), Bet365, DraftKings — all use this. Absence = unrecognizable platform.

PATTERN 02

Probability % (or equivalent odds) as the main number

On every card and detail screen. Even Bet365 makes live odds the dominant card element. It is the market's "price" and "game state" at once.

PATTERN 03

Probability chart over time

From a simple line (Manifold) to candlestick (Kalshi). DraftKings Predictions added charting for the same reason. Movement = engagement.

3 Key differences

DIFF 01

Regulation defines the product

Kalshi = CFTC + US fiat + $1.5B annualized 2026. Polymarket = global + crypto (USDC). DraftKings = CFTC + 38 states. For a global non-US product, Polymarket and Futuur are the structural analogs.

DIFF 02

Sports vs events: sector is bifurcating

Kalshi 89% of revenue from sports. Polymarket sports >60% open interest Oct 2025. Our events-first thesis targets the underserved direction — at lower volume in the near term.

DIFF 03

CLOB vs AMM vs fixed odds

Polymarket + Kalshi = order book (CLOB). Azuro = decentralized AMM. Bet365 = house-set fixed odds. For cold start, CLOB has the liquidity chicken-and-egg problem. [?]

1 Mechanism that won't work

❌ Do not use

Regulatory badges — FSCS / SIPC / FDIC / CFTC

Revolut, Robinhood, Kalshi display official regulatory shields. We don't, and won't (these are legal obligations, not decorations). Similar elements without the real backing will destroy trust the moment a user checks. FTX built a "bank-like look" without bank-like guarantees — the outcome is known.

Alternative: on-chain transparency — "All settlements are on-chain. You can verify every transaction."

UX Patterns

Interaction architecture

The key behavioral pattern of our audience is Event-triggered arrival: the user comes when something happens. How we greet them "arriving from a news story" determines activation and the first bet.

5 Fundamentally different patterns

✓ Chosen for MVP

Story-driven Discovery

Event = narrative unit: context + why it matters now + what the market says + resolution conditions → bet CTA.

Where: nowhere in PM fully. Partially — Metaculus (question descriptions), The Athletic (sports + context).

When condition X

Event Feed

Algorithmic feed of cards. User scrolls, stops on what catches their eye.

Where: Polymarket, Kalshi, Twitter, TikTok. Breaks with <20 markets and for new users.

Retention phase

Portfolio / Position-first

First screen — open positions, P&L, deadlines. New markets in a separate tab.

Where: Robinhood, Binance. Empty state for new users = demotivation.

Onboarding

Guided Challenge

"Bet of the day" — one choice, two buttons, game loop. Minimum cognitive load.

Where: Duolingo, HQ Trivia. Gets boring after 5–7 sessions.

✗ Does not fit

Market Board

Table of all markets: price, volume, 24h change. Filters are the main navigation.

Where: Kalshi advanced, Bloomberg, Binance. New users leave immediately. Kills emotional connection to the event.

Why Story-driven — 3 reasons

REASON 01 — JTBD J2

Direct match with JTBD J2

"Follow events with a real stake" — this is about context, not numbers. The user arrives from a news story and wants to understand what's happening. Story-driven gives that context inside the product.

REASON 02 — COMPETITOR GAP

Closes the market's main gap

No competitor explains why the price is what it is or what will affect the outcome. Markets are isolated questions without context. This is our differentiator.

REASON 03 — TRUST

Builds trust without regulatory badges

Clear event description + resolution conditions + source = the platform knows what it's talking about. Trust through content transparency — our alternative to FSCS/SIPC.

Condition X for Event Feed: >30 active markets and the user has already placed their first bet. Story-driven for first contact → Feed for return visits.

Hypotheses — if / then / because

H1 · Activation
Fiat on-ramp will increase activation
If we allow card top-up without a Web3 wallet from the first screen after registration
Then % of users who placed a first bet within 24 hours will exceed 40%
Because the main barrier for News Junkie is needing MetaMask and USDC before the first bet. Polymarket addresses this via MoonPay/Transak but doesn't treat it as a UX priority.
Sources: aarrr.md · Activation "Fiat first" · competitive-analysis.md Q1
H2 · Activation
Story-driven UX will increase new user conversion to first bet
If each event has a narrative unit: context + what the market says + resolution conditions
Then conversion from first visit to bet will be higher than Polymarket / Kalshi
Because no competitor provides context inside the product — the News Junkie arrives "from a news story" and doesn't understand isolated questions without background.
Sources: research.md "No narrative around events" · ux-patterns.md Story-driven · competitive-analysis.md
H3 · Revenue
Fee on win (not on entry) reduces early churn
If the fee is taken on win (~2%) rather than on entry
Then early churn after the first bet will be lower
Because "paid and lost" is the most painful scenario. "Pay only when you earned" is psychologically softer. Option B.
Sources: aarrr.md · Revenue "Option B" · Polymarket fee model
H4 · Trust
Concrete funds protection promise will increase deposit completion
If the first deposit screen shows: "Your USDC are stored 1:1. We never lend them without your permission"
Then deposit completion rate will increase
Because the main fear of a new fintech user is "what happens to my money." Coinbase solves it exactly this way and scores C2: 5/5.
Sources: benchmark-trust.md · Coinbase · screens/coinbase-trust-signals-mobile.png
H5 · Referral
Share card after resolution will drive >20% organic traffic
If after every resolution a share card with the result is auto-generated
Then >20% of new users will arrive via sharing
Because "I told you so" is a powerful social instinct. Winning begs to be shared. Robinhood and Spotify Wrapped confirmed this pattern in other verticals.
Sources: aarrr.md · Referral "Win share" · ux-patterns.md Social sharing
H6 · Trust
Fee transparency before confirmation reduces complaints and churn
If every bet confirmation shows the exact fee amount before submission
Then complaints and refund requests will be lower, NPS higher
Because hidden fees are the #1 cause of negative fintech reviews. Robinhood made "Commission-Free" their headline message and earned C3: 5/5.
Sources: benchmark-trust.md · Robinhood · aarrr.md Revenue
H7 · Retention — NEW from v_refresh
A designed post-resolution loss screen reduces churn after the first losing bet
If after a losing bet the user sees: "here's what happened, here's why the market moved, here's a similar market open right now"
Then day-7 retention after a first-bet loss will be meaningfully higher than competitors
Because no competitor designs the post-resolution loss moment. The first-bet loss is the single biggest churn driver in prediction markets. Context + a clear next action transforms a dead-end into a re-engagement loop.
Sources: aarrr.md · Retention "first-bet loss" · competitive-analysis.md gap table · v_refresh NEW
