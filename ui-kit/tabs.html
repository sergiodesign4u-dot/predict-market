Three tab families, and this page showed one of them

The strip above is not the strip. This file draws three separate tab families and until 2026-08-10 the page stood only the richest: .tabs stands on 9 screens and .ptabs on 2, and neither had ever been drawn in the kit. A reader comparing a tab against a tab was comparing one of three against nothing.

The plain strip, .tabs, from active-bets.html: two links, and the state is aria-current="page" rather than a class. It is the whole of the My Bets switch, Active against History, and it navigates: each tab is a different URL, so nothing is hidden and shown, the page changes.

IT WORE role="tablist" AND role="tab" UNTIL 2026-08-11 AND OWNED NOTHING. A tablist owns elements with role="tab"; here the tabs were one level down inside the links, so the relationship was broken on 9 screens per tree. Two more things were true at once: there is no role="tabpanel" anywhere in that family, in either tree, so the tabs controlled nothing; and they navigate to another document, which a tab does not do. Moving the role up onto the anchor would have fixed the ownership and left a tab with no panel that leaves the page. So the roles came OFF, the container is a <nav> so its label still names something, and the state is the idiom this product already writes 1,228 times. Backlog 89.

AND THE OPPOSITE HAPPENED TO A DIFFERENT STRIP ON 2026-08-16, WHICH IS WHY BOTH SENTENCES BELONG ON ONE PAGE. That pass removed the role from the strip that could not own one; it did not look at the other two bars in this file, and both were still declaring role="tablist" over children with 0 role="tab" and 0 aria-selected in either tree. .rules-tabs is the one of the three that CAN own the role, because it is two real <button>s over two real panels with a script that swaps them, so it has role="tab", aria-selected and role="tabpanel" now, kept in step by the same handler that was already toggling the class. .ed-tabbar and .ptab-bar are <label for> over hidden radios, and a label is not a tab because a label is not focusable, so the role came off both and their panels lost role="tabpanel" with it. The third of the three was missed by the sweep that fixed the first two and was caught by re-running the check, which is the only way a role can be found: nothing in a stylesheet can see one.

The radio names and ids are suffixed per cell. A radio group is keyed by name and a label reaches its input by id, so two theme cells sharing either would be one group across both: pressing a label in daylight would move the dark cell. That is the kit's id rule arriving through a second door, and it is the reason this specimen is not a copy of the screen's attribute values.

The one place the system's single ring could not land

base.css draws the ring once, on the element that HAS focus, and the element that has focus here is the RADIO, which this file parks at left:-9999px. So the indicator was painted off the left edge of the document, and a person tabbing into the tab strip saw nothing at all.

The radio is still focusable, which is the part that matters: it is offscreen rather than removed, so the keyboard still reaches it. What was missing was only the sign that it had. The ring is drawn on the LABEL now, which is the thing a person can see.

The profile strip needed the same answer with the input hidden a different way. There the radio is a 1px box at zero opacity, so the ring is drawn in the right place and then painted at zero alpha, which is the same nothing arrived at from the other direction. Its bar is a scroller and the pill fills its content box, so the offset turns inward there, exactly as it does in the filter panel.

The range rail and the comment sorter are one chip

Measured 2026-08-04: the two agree on padding, radius, size, height, ground and selected ground down to the pixel, and disagreed on the two things left, the face and the weight. One rail was mono at 400 rising to 600 when chosen; the sorter was the body face. Two files, one control, and neither file could take the other without styling another component's classes.

Both are chip's rail face now. What stayed here is the row: the sorter's markup stands in the comments file and its styling has always stood here, so its press stands here too, next to the hover it answers rather than in the other file.

Both selected grounds in this file were a colour function at 16 per cent, which IS a rung and was still off the ladder, because a value spelled as a function is not a token and does not get the theme. On graphite nothing moved; on chalk they now step up like every other brass tint and the label reads 5.72:1 there.

The press, and it is worth most where there is no hover

Every tab in this file was missing it. A tab is a quiet control, so it settles onto the one held-down role in the system, and each press mirrors the hover it answers so the two sit at equal specificity and source order hands the pointer to the press.

It is worth most on a phone, where there is no hover at all: until it arrived, a tap on a tab said nothing until the panel underneath had already changed.

What it does with width

One query, at DESK, and it takes the FRAME off the chart's range group and not a pixel off its buttons. Measured on a coarse pointer at 320, 360, 390, 640 and 1280, every .ed-range chip is exactly 44 x 44, which is the touch floor base.css declares once for the whole family. The row was 54 and the ten pixels were never the buttons: 4px of padding and a hairline a side, 44 + 8 + 2, and the group is 198 wide for the same arithmetic, 4 x 44 + 3 x 4 + 8 + 2. Below the rung the well, the edge and the corner go and the row is 44, the group 182.

The four chips still read as one control with the frame gone, because the chosen one carries a brass ground and the other three carry none: a segmented control is told apart by its selection, and the box around it is what a desk has room for. Anything below 44 is the floor's decision and not this file's, and a component that shrank under it would be the .btn-md defect again, the same control at 48 under a mouse and 47 under a finger.

The rule

A switcher needs no script. A radio, a label and a sibling selector, and the state lives in the document rather than in a variable.

Hide the input where the ring can still be drawn. Offscreen is focusable and invisible, and it takes the indicator with it, so the indicator moves to the label.

Two strips that measure identically are one control. The face and the weight were the only disagreements, and neither had an argument.

The anti-rule

Never park a focusable element offscreen and leave the ring on it. The indicator was painted at -9999px, which is a keyboard user seeing nothing.

Never measure a tabbed screen without cycling the tabs. Three of four panels read 0x0 at both widths and all three are correct.

Never write a floor as a list of tags. Three of these four strips take the touch floor and the fourth is a label rather than a button.
