/* THE ROW IS THE CONTROL, and this file never said so. On 14 screens a position or a notification
   is an <a> wrapped straight round the card, and the whole tap target of My Bets and Notifications
   answered neither the pointer nor the finger. The anchor is the subject and the .pos inside it is
   the surface that changes, which is the shape .related-list already uses. The row comes up to
   meet the pointer: --bg-control-hover is lighter than --bg-card-quiet in BOTH themes, so the
   direction of the state is one decision and not two. The question takes the brass a linked
   question takes everywhere else. */
.app-case a:has(>.pos):hover>.pos{background:var(--bg-control-hover);border-color:var(--border-brass-hover)}
.app-case a:has(>.pos):hover .pos-q{color:var(--text-brass)}
/* PRESS, AND THE GROUND ALONE WOULD BE A LIE HERE. --bg-pressed is the system's held-down stone,
   and it was measured against a control that rests on --bg-control. This row does not: it rests on
   --bg-card-quiet, which is one step DEEPER, so the press lightens it by four bytes per channel in
   the Vault (#15171b to #191b1f) and in daylight the two roles resolve to the same chalk-880 and
   the press renders as nothing at all. So it is paid for out of depth, which is what this row is
   made of: the outer drop goes and the lit lip stays, and a lifted stone lies down flat under the
   finger in both themes. The brass edge and the brass question are restated rather than left to
   the hover above, because a touch screen never hovers and this row is read on a phone. Equal
   specificity to the hover (0,4,1), so this has to sit after it.
   What the system does not have is a quiet card's own held-down ground, --bg-card-quiet-pressed:
   one shared role cannot serve both a control and a surface that already rests below it. */
.app-case a:has(>.pos):active>.pos{background:var(--bg-pressed);border-color:var(--border-brass-hover);box-shadow:inset 0 1px 0 var(--edge-groove-light)}
.app-case a:has(>.pos):active .pos-q{color:var(--text-brass)}
.app-case .pos[aria-label="Portfolio summary"] .pos-fig:first-child b{color:var(--text-brass)}
