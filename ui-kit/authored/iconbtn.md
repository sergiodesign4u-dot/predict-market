# iconbtn

## Sources

- `components/header.css`, where this control lived until 2026-08-06. It was never wrong there in the way `.toggle` was wrong inside `components/filters.css`: it was already one class with two modifiers, gathered onto one rest declaration, one hover and one press by backlog S17. What it did not have was a file, so the five other icon buttons in the product had nowhere to arrive.
- `ui-kit/docs/atoms.md`, which is why it has one now. The map counts **908 placements of a press whose whole content is a mark**, drawn in six files under six names, and this is the atom they come to.
- `ui-kit/_worn.py`, the census, which files it as an **action**: pressing it does a thing, and nothing is carried between presses.
- `ui-kit/docs/backlog.md` S17, closed 2026-08-03, which is the pass that made the circle one declaration instead of two.

## Purpose

A press whose whole content is a mark. Where a label would say the same thing in a word, use `button`; this exists for the places a band has no room for a word and the mark is unambiguous on its own: add funds, swap the balance, open the menu, dismiss a sheet, bookmark an event.

## Anatomy

- `.icon-btn` - the ghost circle: 36 by 36, a transparent ground, a hairline edge, a pill radius, the mark handed the current ink, and since 2026-08-07 a centred `text-align` with no underline - the two values the `<button>` tag had been supplying for free, found when Favorites in the header became an `<a>`. Neither draws anything, which is the argument for writing them down rather than against it. It is deliberately the absence of a control: a band of eight filled chips is a band nobody can read. **It is also the header band's SKIN rather than this atom's own idea**, which counting settled on 2026-08-07: all 242 of them stand in `<header>`, every icon button anywhere else in the product carries a face, and three controls that are not this atom wear the same thing to the byte - the two `<summary>` disclosures and the How-it-works pill. What they share is written once as a token and three times as a rule, for the reason under Rule below.
- `.icon-btn.bal-swap` - the same circle at 32, for the pair that sits inside the balance pill where 36 would not fit.
- `.icon-btn.bal-add` - the one icon button in the product that carries the primary action's brass plate, at 32. It opens Add funds, which is the header's own commitment.

## When to use

In a band, a bar or a corner, for an action a mark states completely. The mark must carry an `aria-label`, because a control with no text has no accessible name and the mark is not one.

Never for an action a person has to think about. A brass plate on a 32px circle is as much emphasis as this control can carry, and there is exactly one of those.

## Rule

**The box is the target, not the padding around it.** 36 under a fine pointer, 44 under a coarse one, written on the control itself, because target size follows the POINTER and not the viewport: a touch laptop at 1280 needs the same 44 a phone does. An invisible hit area stretched over a smaller circle is a fix nobody in this repo can measure, which is the argument `ui-kit/authored/toggle.md` makes about the switch.

**A region says where it sits, never what it looks like.** `components/header.css` keeps `.app-header .left > .icon-btn{display:none}`, because hiding a control in a band is the band's decision, and it keeps nothing else about this control. That sentence is the whole of the atom migration, and it is written in `ui-kit/docs/atoms.md`.

**A surface may own a SKIN, and a skin is a set of tokens.** The band's four quiet controls are three different atoms, so no atom can hold what they agree on: a fourth file drawing `.icon-btn`, `summary` and `.btn` is three more file-slots on the map and a shared class is that file with a name on it. The two `<summary>` disclosures are the proof - a summary is neither `button` nor `iconbutton` - so what is shared has to be a VALUE, and each of the three files goes on saying, in its own rule, that its control stands there. `ui-kit/docs/backlog.md` S48.

**A face answers the pointer with the accent unless the accent is already carrying a state.** That is what settled S45 on 2026-08-07, and it is measured rather than argued: a SAVED tile's mark is `rgb(215, 172, 83)` and the social row's hovered mark is `rgb(215, 172, 83)`, the same value, so giving the tile the loud hover would paint an unsaved bookmark the exact ink that means saved. Two of this atom's six faces spend brass on a state; the ones that spend none may spend it on a pointer.

## Anti-rule

Never draw one from the `button` family. `button` gives a quiet control `--bg-control` and a 10px radius; this is a transparent ground, a hairline and a pill. Painting one from the other puts a filled chip in a band whose entire material is the absence of one.

Never add a third face here without the measurement. Five more kinds of icon button are still drawn in five other files - the sheet close, the bookmark, a comment's action, an event's action row, the toast close - and each arrives on its own evidence, with its own before-and-after at zero difference. Guessing which of them is "the same" is the mistake this atom exists to stop.

Seen: `ui-kit/docs/atoms.md`, and `ui-kit/docs/defects.md`, which records what the first reading of the family refused.

## States

- `button.icon-btn @button-outcome-row` - **The ghost circle, and one block here is four controls.** Measured across all four states in both themes, the same answer is given by `.bal-swap` (the Portfolio / Cash swap), by `.desk-only` (the control that exists only above the breakpoint) and by the plain circle of the utility row. That is not an accident anybody keeps in step: the rest, the hover and the press are one declaration each, written after measuring the shape across 105 screens at 360 and 1440. Photographed outside the header, where nothing else in the band is around it.
- `button.kit-here.icon-btn.bal-add @.app-header` - **The plus that opens Add funds, and it does NOT merge with the block above, which is the useful half of the measurement.** It is the one icon button in the product carrying the primary action's brass plate, and it carries the same three-step `components/button.css` uses: rest lights the plate from the top left, hover takes the lit role to both stops, press flips the angle so the light falls to the bottom right.
- `button.icon-btn.icon-btn-photo.sheet-close @dialog.signin-dialog` - **The face for a photograph, and it is one block for all 333 dismissals in the product.** A scrim ground at 34 per cent, the on-photo edge and the on-photo ink, and its two states deepen the scrim rather than tinting brass: a brass tint on a photograph is a colour nobody chose competing with one nobody controls. 34 at rest, 55 hovered, 72 held.
- `button.icon-btn.icon-btn-photo.icon-btn-ring-strong.sheet-cl @dialog.hiw-dialog` - **The same disc on the how-it-works head, and the only thing that splits it off is the RING.** `--text-strong` rather than brass, because `.hiw-glow` puts a brass radial in the corner the ring lands in. The ring belongs to the overlay, so the reason is written on the `dialog` page; the split is here because the face is measured here.
- `button.icon-btn.icon-btn-bare.bookmark-btn @card-binary` - **The bare face, and it is the one face of this atom with no ground and no edge at all.** It sits on a card's caption row beside an event photograph, where a circle would be a second object competing with the picture, so what answers the pointer is the MARK: quiet at rest, brass under a pointer, and filled brass when the event is saved. That last one is a state of the EVENT and not of the control, which is why it is `aria-pressed` and not a hover. Its whole 44 target is invisible and pulled back out of the flow by a negative margin, because an untouchable box that spreads the row it sits in is this atom's problem wherever it stands.
- `button.icon-btn.icon-btn-tile @event-detail` - **The tile, and it is the only icon button in the product with a 10px corner rather than a pill.** Three of them sit in a row over the event head, and three circles read as a control group where three tiles read as a toolbar. It is the one face with a ground of its own on an ordinary surface, `--bg-control`, because the head behind it is a photograph and a transparent tile would vanish into it. It keeps its own hover, which brightens the ink and takes the edge to `--bevel-strong` rather than tinting brass, so the face has to put the atom's tint back and repeat the press at its own weight. **Why it may not tint brass was answered on 2026-08-07: this is the one face that already spends brass on a STATE.** A saved bookmark's mark is `rgb(215, 172, 83)` and the social row's hovered mark is the same value, so the loud hover would paint an unsaved bookmark the ink that means saved. Its mark is 18 and its ink is `--text-muted`: both were decided on 2026-08-06 rather than inherited from a browser, `ui-kit/docs/backlog.md` S44. **And the saved state costs something nobody had priced**: at (0,4,0) it outranks the hover as well as the press, so a saved tile answers no pointer at all, which is S38's shape on a second axis.
- `button.icon-btn.icon-btn-small.toast-close @toast` - **The circle at 24, and it is a SIZE and not a sixth face.** Transparent, hairline, pill, exactly the header's, with the X drawn as two bars in `currentColor` so the mark moves with the ink and the two cannot drift. The bar is 8 and not the 12 the sheet close draws, because 12 in 24 would be an X pressed against its own edge. It used to disagree with the other 388 outlined circles on its hover, brightening its edge where they tint their ground; four against 388, so the four were corrected. It keeps the ink lift, because it rests at `--text-muted` and has somewhere to go.
- `a.icon-btn.icon-btn-lift @footer (2)` - **The sixth face, and the one the map had in the wrong atom.** 525 social marks in the footer of all 105 screens, filed as `navitem` because they GO somewhere. Measured in the browser they are this control: `--bg-control`, a 1px hairline, a 10px corner, a centred flex box at 28 and an 18px mark, which is what `.icon-btn-tile` measures and is nothing the other two navitems measure. It shares the rest declaration with the tile in one selector rather than repeating it. **Where the two part is the pointer**: this one lifts two pixels, tints its ground with 20 per cent of the action role and takes a brass edge, then settles back on press because a held link should not hover in mid air. The tile brightens its ink instead. **27 against 525, and the majority was not what decided it.** S45 closed on 2026-08-07 with a rule that is neither one hover per atom nor one per surface: a face answers the pointer with the accent unless the accent is already carrying a state of its own. This row carries none, so it may be loud; the tile carries `aria-pressed` in the same brass, so it may not. The mark keeps its stroke: these are brand shapes and this face hands out no ink at all.
