The other five figures are left standing as the 2026-08-10 reading rather than turned by arithmetic, because a count is computed or it is dated, and a number nobody measured today is a number this page may not print as a live one.

Nothing here is red, including the error

DESIGN.md decides it once for the system: green and red are outcome semantics, YES and NO. A declined card is not a NO. So .inline-error is a hairline on the control ground with the primary ink, and the whole error signal is where it stands: in place, beside the thing that failed.

The reconcile box is the exception that proves the rule, and it is not an exception. It DOES take colour, green on a win and neutral on a loss, and the colour is the OUTCOME of a bet rather than the severity of a message. That is the same token doing the same job it does everywhere else in the product. Since 2026-08-08 it says so in its own class, .rec-won and .rec-lost, rather than being told by the dialog around it.

A dashed edge means "not here yet", and it is used exactly twice

The widget slot and the wait are the only dashed boxes in the system. Both are saying the same thing: something will be here and it is not here now. Everything else in the product that is absent is a skeleton, which draws the SHAPE of what is coming; a dashed box is for the case where the shape is somebody else's to decide.

110 widget slots on 105 screens, read 2026-08-10 and not walked again since, because the deposit dialog is in the markup of every screen and its payment widget belongs to a provider this product does not draw. That is the honest way to hold a hole.

Two of these faces were written against a CONTAINER, and both are classes now

The kit found them, on 2026-08-08, by rendering them. .widget-box had its box unscoped and its FACE under dialog.app-dialog, so the specimen on this page rendered transparent at a 0px corner with the wrong ink against the product's control ground at 10px with the muted one. All 110 stand in a dialog, so no screen was wrong and this page was the only place it showed.

.protect had two real faces and no class for either: a TRUST tint inside a dialog and a BRASS tint in a page, kept apart by nothing but the fact that a <dialog> sits outside the .app-case wrapper. Measured on 2026-08-08: 113 of the 116 stood in a dialog and 3 in a page. So the trust face is the default and the page face is .protect-page, and neither depends on where the element happens to land. This page had been showing the minority face while its own table described the majority one.

It is the same correction the money field took an hour earlier and the sixth time this repository has made it: a face is a class, and a container may only say where the control sits. Measured after: the kit's specimen and the product's field are the same colour, the same edge and the same corner, in both faces.

The box carries no heading, no mark and no control. It is a sentence with a box around it, and it stands on 114 of the 115 painted documents, read 2026-08-19 and 105 when this line was written, because the sentence is the same one every time.

AND THE PAGE FACE LOST ITS OWN MEASURE ON FIVE OF ITS SIX PLACEMENTS, 2026-08-19. .protect.protect-page still declares max-width:var(--measure) in notice.css, and on wallet.html that cap is the whole reason the box reads as a notice: there the sentence is dropped into .cat-main, a column sized for cards and 150ch wide, and it ran 117 characters on one line without it. On the five document pages the column IS the measure now, so patterns/browse-shell.css takes the cap off with .read-col .protect.protect-page{max-width:none} and three more declarations that hand the box the document's own type. A second measure inside a reading column is not a narrower notice, it is a second left edge: measured at 1440 before it came off, one document drew a 600px column, a 346px box and 409px of prose inside its sections, three widths on a page whose grey twin has one. The role did not change and the placement did, which is why the declaration survives on the majority rather than being deleted by one.

And a specimen on this page cannot be put inside a win dialog, so two of the component's three faces could not be stood by anybody: they are above, side by side, for the first time. A face that cannot be stood cannot be checked.

The rule

A notice explains and does not act. Five of the six carry no control at all. The sixth, the permission banner, is an ASK, and its two buttons come from button rather than from here.

Severity is the edge and the place, never a new colour. Solid means it happened, dashed means it has not arrived, and a failure is said where it failed.

The anti-rule

Never make the error red. Red is NO in this product, and a declined card is not an outcome.

Never draw a control inside a notice's file. The permission banner's buttons are the button family's; this file owns the row they sit in and nothing else.

Never use a dashed box for content this product owns. A shape we can draw is a skeleton. A dash is an admission that somebody else fills the hole.
