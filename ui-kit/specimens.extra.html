Page frame and text reset

The page frame

The graphite canvas, the two-stone slab under it and the content plate you are reading on. Everything a screen stands on before a single component is loaded.

The text reset is three lines: margins off and border-box on, the body font and text colour inherited by everything, and one rule that makes a control inherit the body font instead of the system one.

The two icons are the other base rule: a sprite icon is filled with the current colour, a drawn one is stroked. Same class, two shapes of source.
