The system, as it stands today

51 stylesheets in components/, 5,651 lines, reached through components/index.css. Two token levels, primitive and semantic, and a light theme that exists to prove the semantic one. That is the whole system, and it is what paints this page.

What is being built here

In order, and each step ends in something a person reads rather than in something a machine re-runs.

1. Census

Five anchor screens and their states, walked in a browser at two widths in both themes, computed values rather than written rules: which forms actually exist.

2. Levels

Atom, molecule, organism, declared once in an inventory with a reason each. A level is one plus the highest level of what a component contains, and it is a decision rather than a reading, because a component built out of its own class names reads as containing nothing.

3. Consolidation

One pass where a value is allowed to change: the radius scale, the hairline, the icon stroke, the size ramp. Every change measured before and after, and written down.

4. The pages

One hand-written page per component: a live specimen, its states in both themes side by side, its classes, its rule and its anti-rule, and the screens it stands on. A page is a page, not the output of a generator.

5. One audit

Contrast, overflow, focus, two widths, two themes. A report, not a gate.

The five anchors

Event feed, event detail, active bets, deposit and sign in, with their loading, empty, error and logged-out variants: 41 screens of the 106. Between them they carry the header in both auth states, the bottom nav, the footer, the card, the chip family, the filters, the tabs, the YES and NO pair, the bet panel, the dialog sheet, the fields, the toasts, the skeletons, the empty and error blocks.
