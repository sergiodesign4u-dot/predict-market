The Loss-Prone User

Not a persona, a behavioral pattern. This pattern can emerge from ANY of the three personas above, especially Alex.

Revised Behavioral Sequence

01 First WIN → overconfidence ← trigger is the WIN, not the first loss
02 Escalating bet sizes → systematic losses
03 Loss-chasing → catastrophic exit
04 Stops only via external force (partner confrontation, lawsuit, account block) - not self-regulation

Three Behavioral Archetypes

ArchetypePatternEvidence
Loss Chaser First win → overconfidence → escalation → chasing K.A. (Kalshi, $10K in 8 days), Lorenzo Miro (Polymarket, $1.7K), "Poly Hell" author ($10K more after losing $7.5K)
Casual Experimenter Small deposit (<$10), dormant after first loss or event ends ~50% of 2M Polymarket wallets made/lost <$10 (PYMNTS 2026) - MODAL behavior
Platform-Betrayal Quitter Won or should have won, platform failed them. Writes Trustpilot review. Wants to return. Kalshi 1.9/5, Polymarket 1.4/5, 90% one-star

Design Implications

→ WIN screen needs design attention equal to loss screen - early wins trigger overconfidence
→ POST-LOSS screen: intervention point before chasing begins - speak to Casual Experimenter first (re-entry path)
→ Platform betrayal (frozen funds, wrong payouts, resolution disputes) = #1 documented churn driver
→ No competitor designs either moment today
Evidence

PMC/NCB 2024 study: 52.5% of subjects in loss condition continued playing vs 47.5% in control.
