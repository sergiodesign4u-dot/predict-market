Information Architecture Prediction Market Platform MVP · 2026

IA Overview

A mobile-first Web3 prediction market where users bet YES/NO on real-world events using stablecoins. The differentiator is clarity for new users - everyone always understands what they're doing and what happens next.

Screen Tree

Level 0 and Level 1 screens

Level 0 = bottom nav destinations (Events, My Bets, Favorites, Portfolio). Level 1 = one tap from Level 0. Category pages (Politics / Crypto / Culture / General) are browse screens under Events. The bet is an inline panel on Event Detail, not a separate screen; Sign In / Deposit are in-page dialogs.

Navigation hierarchy: Level 0 bottom-nav slots branch to Level 1 screens. Header (Notifications bell, Favorites heart, Wallet, How It Works), invoked dialogs/overlays, and deferred screens follow below as distinct muted groups.

Navigation

Bottom navigation - 4 slots

Derived from the follow-stake-watch-resolve-reputation loop, not from any competitor tab bar. Money, help, and other users' profiles are second-level - not why the user opens the app.
