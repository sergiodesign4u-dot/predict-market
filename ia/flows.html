Overview · Full Sitemap

Outcome color (nodes are colored by outcome):
green = happy-path start + the job-closed success terminal  ·  red = a true dead-end (no path back to the goal)  ·  grey = neutral: intermediate screens, decisions, and loading/empty/error nodes that recover.
In these four flows every error and churn terminal (T6, T7, T9, T10, T12, T16 and the rest) recovers with an arrow back into the flow, so none are true dead-ends; the red class is defined but unused.
[Square brackets] = screens from IA/sitemap.md  ·  {Diamonds} = decisions  ·  ([Stadiums]) = terminal states
Success terminals: T14/mjDone, fj2Done, fj5Done, sj1Done.
Error/churn terminals T1-T3, T5-T12, T13a, T13b, T15-T16 each have at least one recovery edge. T4 retired as terminal (replaced by escalation-path edge in FJ5).
Coverage note: Flows are drawn for the main job and key related jobs only. Jobs and screens without a dedicated flow (SJ2, Notifications list, Bet History tab) are covered passively or inside existing screens - this is by design, not a gap.
Traces to the CJM To-Be (user-research/docs/cjm-to-be.md, Alex x MJ). The 7 To-Be steps: 1. arrive on a live event, not a signup; 2. understand the market before any account; 3. tap YES/NO and see the bet intent before signing in; 4. sign in and fund with one funds-safety line; 5. confirm with the price reconciled; 6. follow your bet with live context; 7. resolution designed both ways. Per-flow mapping is under each diagram below.
Deferred polish (P3 - later pass): P3-3 (HIW static vs fetch clarification), P3-4 (first-visit vs return-visit layout naming on Event Feed), P3-6 (minimal Wallet withdrawal flow), P3-7 (HIW as optional step in FJ2).

MJ - Main Job
When an event I follow is approaching resolution, I want a real stake on the outcome
The primary job. Entry from news/notification or Event Feed. Two persona branches at the gate (News Junkie / Crypto Native). S5 AMM price reconcile before bet execution. Success: T14 - bet placed, user follows the event.

FJ2 - Functional Job 2
When I see an event and its probability, I want to understand why the market prices it that way
The "understand the odds" job. Entry from Event Feed percentage or an article. Two outcomes: watcher (understood, not betting) or proceeds to the bet (the inline panel on Event Detail).

Traces to CJM To-Be step 2: understand the market before any account (explain the number: inline probability + one-line why + the story), feeding step 3 (bet intent) for the user with an edge.

FJ5 + EJ3 - Functional Job 5 + Emotional Job 3
When an event I bet on resolves with a loss, I want to exit consciously without the impulse to chase
The loss-chasing intervention flow. Two entry paths: G1 (direct notification - 1 tap) and manual (Active Bets). The resolution note is the default beat. Escalation path routes through a friction node (pause). Reserved session-aware hook placed but not built. Conscious exit leads to Event Feed.

SJ1 - Social Job 1
When I win a bet on an event everyone talked about, I want to easily show that to my circle
The win-share flow. Two entry paths: G1-equivalent win notification (1 tap) and manual via My Bets History tab. Default next action is share / mark the moment (SJ1 primary). "See next events" is a deliberate secondary CTA per F5 (overconfidence risk). T13a and T13b are distinct recovery terminals.
