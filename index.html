Yonder

A prediction market platform

A mobile-first web product where a person bets YES or NO on real-world events using stablecoins. Outcomes resolve against reality, not against opinion. The bet this project makes is better clarity and onboarding than the existing markets, for people who follow the news and are not traders.

What you are holding is a finished design deliverable and its documentation. There is no application code, no server, no database and no build step, and that is deliberate: every screen below is a static file you can open. The panel on the left is the twelve stages that produced it, in order.
