Stage 06 · Concept

Signal the visual language

A dark, high-contrast register for a spectator with an opinion. Electric violet leads, a single acid-lime highlight, green and red kept for outcomes, real event photography as a small thumbnail. Every decision below traces to an attribute (A1 to A5 in concept.md) or to the designer taste, never to a reflex.
