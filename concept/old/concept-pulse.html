Stage 07 · Concept · Pulse (alternative direction)

Pulse the visual language

The night register turned up: a warm near-black void, hot magenta as the single brand signal, and wave lines drawn softly into each card corner. A confident, high-energy read for a spectator with an opinion. Green and red stay reserved for outcomes, real event photography rides along as a small thumbnail. The alternative to Signal, sharing its structure but not its colour.

Base: Night desk on deep void Texture: wave lines, corner-masked Accent: Magenta Max, hot pop Restraint: accent = action only Binary card: B · Multi: D

Typography

Space Grotesk for the voice, DM Sans for the read

A characterful geometric grotesque against a warm humanist workhorse, paired on a contrast axis. Space Grotesk carries questions and numbers with edge; DM Sans carries the why line and meta, quietly; IBM Plex Mono tags the figures. A2 A3
